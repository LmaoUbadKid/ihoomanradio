# 🎧 iHooman Radio

A cozy little web radio built for every mood.

Instead of choosing individual songs, just pick a station, press play, and let the music flow. Each station is personally curated with tracks I genuinely love.

## ✨ Features

- 🎵 **6 Handpicked Radio Stations**
  - Each station has its own vibe and carefully selected playlist.
  - No random recommendations—just my personal favourites.

- 🎨 **Custom Background Themes**
  - Change the background colour to match your aesthetic.
  - Make the player feel like your own.

- 🔊 **Volume Control**
  - Easily adjust the audio level without affecting your system volume.

- 📜 **Tracklist Viewer**
  - See what's currently playing and what's coming up next.

- 🪟 **Mini Player Mode**
  - Minimise the radio into a compact floating player while you browse the rest of the website.

- 📻 **Always-On Experience**
  - Built to feel like an actual radio—just tune in and enjoy.

## 🎶 Stations

- 💪 Gym
- 📚 Study
- 🔒 Focus
- 🎤 Pop
- ☀️ Chill
- 🌙 Late Night

## 🚀 Why I Built This

I wanted music on my website to feel different from a traditional playlist.

Instead of endlessly searching for the perfect song, you simply choose a vibe and let the radio do the rest. It's designed to be something you leave running while studying, working, gaming, or relaxing.
